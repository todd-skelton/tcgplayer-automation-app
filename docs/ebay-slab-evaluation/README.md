# eBay slab pricing: narrow evaluation

**Latest technical follow-up:** The user prefers a free, browser-session approach. [Verified browser API findings](browser-api-findings.md) document working Alt certificate lookup, 214 Ponyta cross-grade records, 194 Hitmonlee cross-grade records, and eBay Product Research's internal search endpoint. Eleven overlapping Ponyta sale prices agreed between Alt and eBay, including two Best Offers. This supersedes the paid-provider-first technical approach below; unattended collection and broad inventory coverage remain untested.

**Recommendation: pursue a review-first slab-pricing feature within this application once a data source explicitly permits the intended pricing use. Give it its own identity, evidence, model, and publication boundaries. Cross-grade modeling is technically worth pursuing. A separate application would duplicate infrastructure without resolving trustworthy comps, sparse data, or usage rights. Automatic repricing is not justified by this evaluation.**

**Developer-account follow-up, September 5, 2026:** A developer login enables API key setup, but does not establish production access to every API or permission for every use. With seller authorization, selling APIs can retrieve your own orders; Browse provides listing discovery, while Marketplace Insights remains restricted. More materially, the current API License Agreement's Restricted Activities list, item 5, restricts using eBay Content to suggest or model prices for items listed on eBay without prior written consent. Its Restricted APIs provisions also explicitly require consent for internal or external pricing tools. This restriction was missed in the initial evaluation and makes the implementation recommendation conditional on suitable permission. A third-party subscription must expressly cover this intended use; access to rows alone does not resolve rights. The developer account is useful for inventory/order integration and checking existing entitlements, but does not by itself close either the market-sales access gap or the pricing-use restriction. [API setup](https://developer.ebay.com/develop/guides/sell/get-started-with-ebay-apis), [Browse](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/api-browse.html), [Fulfillment release notes: two-year order history](https://www.developer.ebay.com/api-docs/sell/fulfillment/static/release-notes.html), [current API agreement](https://developer.ebay.com/join/api-license-agreement).

Observed September 5, 2026 using [pokebash](https://www.ebay.com/usr/pokebash), live Seller Hub Product Research, and the local application. This evaluation created research artifacts only; it did not change listings or application pricing.

The inventory sample contains the first 50 distinct slab listings encountered among 144 live store listings inspected. Those 50 listings represent 49 valuation groups because two Zapdos listings have the same card and grade. This is a convenience sample, not a random or complete inventory census. The sales window was June 7–September 5, 2026 on ebay.com.

The searches returned 800 candidate rows, or 785 after removing the repeated Zapdos query. Eight targets returned no sold results. Six searches had additional pages; only the first 50 rows were captured. Search results contained wrong grades, companies, languages, variants, and bundles. Therefore, 42 searches returning something does **not** mean 84% reliable pricing coverage. A title-screened subset supports the diagnostic below; the entire corpus has not been verified against slab images or certification records. The [sample review](sample-review.md) records the limits for every target.

**The cross-grade experiment provides a useful, limited result.** Four Japanese Terastal Festival Eeveelutions had enough PSA 10 observations to test borrowing grade relationships. For each card, I withheld its PSA 9 prices, fitted the median log(PSA 10 / PSA 9) ratio from the other three cards, and used that ratio with the held-out card's PSA 10 median to predict its PSA 9 median. Matching required the card number, Japanese identity, and explicit PSA grade. Conflicting set names and bundles were deferred. All selected rows had one sale, so grouped listing averages were not expanded into fictitious transactions.

| Japanese card | PSA 10 observations | PSA 9 observations | Observed PSA 10 median | Observed PSA 9 median | Predicted PSA 9 | Absolute error |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Flareon 022/187 | 25 | 3 | $40.00 | $20.61 | $25.79 | 25.1% |
| Vaporeon 031/187 | 19 | 1 | $45.50 | $23.33 | $29.33 | 25.7% |
| Leafeon 003/187 | 29 | 2 | $40.00 | $28.47 | $20.61 | 27.6% |
| Glaceon 041/187 | 23 | 3 | $40.24 | $25.94 | $20.73 | 20.1% |

Prices include reported shipping, exclude tax, and use unweighted medians. The mean absolute percentage error was **24.6%**, versus 71.2% for simply treating the PSA 10 median as the PSA 9 value. That weak baseline shows the value of a grade adjustment; it does not demonstrate superiority over a competent pricing system. Observed PSA 10 premiums ranged from 1.41× to 1.95× even within this small related cohort.

This is a cross-sectional diagnostic, **not a forecast backtest**. It uses the same 90-day window across cards, only nine PSA 9 observations in total, and title-compatible rather than independently certified matches. Different sale dates, shipping destinations, seller quality, and auctions can affect the comparisons. It cannot establish future accuracy, prediction-interval calibration, or applicability to vintage cards and rare graders. It is enough to justify testing a richer model, and enough to reject a universal grade multiplier for unattended pricing.

**Supply can inform competition, but the observed asks cannot safely fill gaps by themselves.**

| Target | Sales evidence | Active supply observed | Implication |
|---|---|---|---|
| Slaking 18/18 PSA 9 | Two sales: $16.00 and $20.72 delivered | Your $100 + $5.17 listing; another PSA 9 at $59.99 + $5.62 with a July 2021 start date | A surviving ask can be far above observed transactions. Flag for review rather than using the ask to lift fair value. |
| Krabby 010/128 CGC 9 | No exact-grade result; one CGC 8 at $14 delivered and one CGC 10 at $22.50 | Grade 7 ask $34.99, grade 8 $29.99 + $8.60, your grade 9 $19 + $5.17; Pristine 10 also present | Nearby-grade sales offer a weak anchor after resolving set-name identity. Asks are not even monotonic across grades. Two sales cannot justify a fitted grade curve. |
| Espathra 081 PSA 8 | No confirmed graded sale in either exact-grade or broader PSA query | Your $14.99 + $5.17; blister PSA 9 asks and a $1,500 PSA 10 ask | There is no defensible sale anchor here. Keep the current price or request research; do not manufacture confidence from the PSA 10 ask. |
| Flareon 022/187 PSA 10 | 25 title-compatible sales; $40 delivered median | Your $50 + $5.17; competing PSA 10 asks include $45.99 + $6.50 | Useful competitive positioning, but the active query also includes raw cards and other graders and has more than one page. |

The Slaking PSA 10 supply also included a $500 auction with zero bids and a $1,400 ask. Neither is a completed-sale price. Listing start dates are context, not proof of continuous exposure at today's price. A single supply snapshot cannot identify demand or reliable time to sell. Track listing histories, prices, availability, and verified sale outcomes before fitting that part of the model. Exclude your own supply from competitor features and distinguish disappearance from a confirmed sale.

**Identity errors are a more immediate obstacle than the choice of regression.** The sole Goldeen CGC 10 result was Spanish, while the target is an English listing. The Makuhita CGC 5 query returned a Pristine 10 with “Low Pop (5)” in its title. Your [Sentomaru listing](https://www.ebay.com/itm/397978107151) says CGC 10 in the title, but its seller description specifies Pristine 10; the structured card-number field incorrectly contains the year. Okidogi comps include two unspecified-label CGC 10 sales around $27 delivered and a Pristine 10 sale at $76.75. The unspecified labels require checking before calling them Gem Mint comps. PSA 1 Gengar sales also illustrate why low grades can have a collector market rather than sit at the bottom of a universal price ladder.

For sparse pricing, use a card-and-variant value plus grade effects learned within grading company, label, language, and a relevant card cohort. Let direct target-grade sales dominate when available; shrink sparse estimates toward relationships observed in related cards. Treat Pristine, Gem Mint, qualifiers, and historical grading scales explicitly. Do not impose one numerical ladder across PSA, CGC, SGC, FCG, and RCG. Supply should affect competitive positioning and, once exposure data exists, sale probability. It should not be treated as additional completed sales.

Each recommendation should display its matched identity, direct comps, borrowed-grade evidence, model contribution, age of evidence, and an uncertainty range. Distinguish direct evidence, modeled estimate, and insufficient evidence. Compare sold delivered prices with delivered asks, then calculate the listing price and seller proceeds using shipping, fees, and seller constraints. Low confidence should produce abstention or review.

**Data access is feasible for a manual pilot; unattended access remains unproven.** Your existing Seller Hub session successfully supplied this sample. eBay documents three years of research history and actual accepted Best Offer prices. That is useful for expanding sparse searches beyond 90 days, with time adjustment. It is a user interface, not an established production feed for this application. [eBay Product Research](https://www.ebay.com/help/selling/selling-tools/product-research?id=4853).

eBay states that Marketplace Insights is restricted and not open to new users; its Buy API documentation also recommends obtaining applicable production approval before significant development. Do not base the project on assumed access to a public sold-price API. [API restriction](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/static/ref-buy-browse-filters.html), [production requirements](https://developer.ebay.com/api-docs/buy/static/buy-requirements.html).

Two documented vendor candidates merit a small coverage trial, not an immediate subscription commitment. PokeTrace advertises individual sold listings on its $98/month Scale plan; its documented fields include grader and grade, but explicit Pristine/Gem Mint separation and shipping completeness need validation. The Card API advertises a $199/month Pro tier with 90-day history and documents sale rows with nullable shipping. No vendor key was obtained, no endpoint coverage was tested, and data licensing/retention rights have not been verified for this use. [PokeTrace pricing](https://poketrace.com/pricing), [listing schema](https://poketrace.com/docs/listings), [grade schema](https://poketrace.com/docs/graded-prices), [The Card API pricing](https://www.thecardapi.com/pricing), [schema](https://www.thecardapi.com/docs).

PriceCharting's documented API provides current values but does not support historic prices or historic sales. That makes it a secondary reference rather than the foundation for an auditable transaction-and-supply model. [PriceCharting API](https://www.pricecharting.com/api-documentation). At an illustrative $30/hour value of time, a $100/month feed alone needs about 3.3 hours of monthly savings before development and maintenance; this evaluation did not measure time savings or return on investment.

**Keep the feature in this repository, but give it a separate domain.** The existing [latest-sales algorithm](../../app/features/pricing/algorithms/getSuggestedPriceFromLatestSales.ts) already combines sales, sibling conditions, and supply. Its [condition normalization](../../app/features/pricing/algorithms/conditionNormalization.ts) uses a time-aware condition model, which is a useful conceptual precedent. However, it depends on TCGplayer conditions and catalog-backed sibling prices. [PricerSku](../../app/core/types/pricing.ts) requires numeric TCGplayer identifiers, and the [orchestrator](../../app/features/pricing/services/pricingOrchestrator.ts) constructs the TCGplayer output converter directly. Slabs should not receive fabricated TCGplayer SKUs or be forced into those condition types.

Create an `ebay-slab-pricing` vertical slice with inventory identity, evidence ingestion, comp review, valuation, and eventually eBay publication. A slab's certification identifies the physical item; its card/variant/grader/grade/label identifies its valuation group. Reuse the application shell, database infrastructure, and proven job/review patterns through composition. Extract shared statistical helpers only when both implementations actually need them. Keep marketplace credentials, schemas, and publication logic separate. Reconsider a separate deployment only if operational or customer requirements later demand independent isolation.

Once data access and pricing-use permission are established, the next useful implementation is a read-only pilot for well-identified PSA and CGC cohorts, preserving a manual lane for sparse and unusual items. Before enabling publication, compare vendor results against these saved Seller Hub examples, resolve sample identities, expand to dated historical sales, and run time-separated and card-held-out evaluations against direct-comps-only and cohort-model baselines. Measure matching precision, error by cohort, interval coverage, abstention rate, and review time. The present 24.6% diagnostic error is a reason to retain review, not a proposed production threshold.

Reproduce the calculations with `node docs/ebay-slab-evaluation/analyze.mjs`. [evidence.json](evidence.json) contains the normalized source rows and query URLs; [results.json](results.json) contains computed metrics and selected row numbers. Full account-page snapshots remain only in ignored `.artifacts/ebay-slab-evaluation`; the normalizer is for those local captures. Quantitative examples are provisional pricing evidence, not instructions to revise a listing.
