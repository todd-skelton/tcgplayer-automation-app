# Cross-grade model evaluation plan

These adoption thresholds are declared before the new evaluation runs, in `app/features/ebay-slab-pricing/model/gradeModelPolicy.json`. They are deliberately stricter than the four-card exploratory diagnostic. No cohort starts enabled.

Compare the direct-sales baseline, a within-cohort median grade ratio, and a compact fitted/shrunk log-ratio model. Card value comes from that card's historical sales. A grade-pair prior comes only from other verified cards in the same game, set, language, edition, finish, stamp, grader and label family. Pair effects are unconstrained; PSA 1 collector behavior and premium grading labels are not merged into a monotonic universal ladder. As target-grade evidence grows, its recency-weighted direct value dominates the borrowed prior.

For each held-out card, freeze model fitting before the calibration period and exclude that card from the learned grade effects. Calibration uses later transactions from other cards; testing uses still-later transactions of the held-out card. Only historical observations already captured before the relevant forecast cutoff may inform a prediction. Duplicate sale events count once. Provider estimates, grouped multi-item averages, uncertain matches and future observations are ineligible. Report dense and target-grade-hidden scenarios separately. The initial four-card diagnostic is reproduced separately from this forward evaluation: its title-compatible single-sale medians were captured after the sales and are not verified certification ground truth.

Adoption requires at least 30 test cards, 200 test transactions, 30 calibration transactions and 30 paired comparisons, at least 50% prediction coverage in the sparse scenario, at least 10% lower mean absolute log error than the ratio baseline, no more than 5% log-error regression against direct comps where both predict, and median relative error no greater than 25%. Calibrated 80% intervals must cover 75–90% of held-out sales without a median high/low ratio above 3. An unsupported grade pair, unverified cohort, unavailable historical snapshot or insufficient evaluation keeps the product in direct/manual mode. Synthetic fixtures verify the algorithm and leak barriers; they never count toward adoption.

The first supplied dataset was captured on September 5, 2026. It cannot by itself establish performance for forecasts made before that capture. The evaluation must report this limitation rather than treat sale dates as proof that those observations were available to the model earlier.
