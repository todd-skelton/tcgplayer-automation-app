# Slab comparable-sale screening

The screening functions are pure and operate on normalized evidence plus a confirmed slab identity. They do not call a provider, database, LLM or price-proximity model. Results are `accepted`, `needs-review` or `rejected`, with field-level reasons and the original evidence/source reference. Price premiums are not statistical reasons to discard a sale.

Structured card fields must agree with the target; missing required identity or specified variant fields require review. Titles can expose a contradiction but matching search words cannot establish a card identity. Explicit language, card number, year, edition, finish, stamp and grade clues are checked. Non-Latin names remain distinct. Bundles and proxies are rejected; holder damage, reprints and ambiguous metadata require review. Multiple graders/labels and numeric grade versus label conflicts remain visible. A nearby “Low Pop (5)” does not change an adjacent `CGC 10` into grade 5. CGC 10 does not imply Gem Mint or Pristine. Unknown provider grade encodings remain unresolved even when a title provides a plausible label. PSA qualifiers are checked in structured fields, labels and titles.

`normalizeSalePrice` distinguishes the reported amount, item-only amount, item plus shipping and total with known buyer fees. It subtracts included shipping/premiums once, then adds the known components once when producing totals. Unknown inclusion flags, shipping, fees and transaction quantity semantics remain unknown. Cross-currency calculation requires an explicitly supplied rate with the sale's date and a named source; no exchange-rate service is introduced. A provider-reported converted amount may be retained as reported evidence, but an undated conversion does not replace a differently denominated native price. If a currency-unknown original price disagrees numerically with the provider's converted field, conversion is unresolved rather than silently selected.

These rules intentionally leave some otherwise plausible sales for review. In particular, Alt currently omits several identity and money fields, and eBay search rows lack structured card identity. A comp reviewer can verify an uncertain match and explicitly enter the item price. Review cannot turn an unconfirmed outcome, unknown date or known identity contradiction into an accepted sale. Totals remain unknown where their components are unknown. Grouped averages retain their quantity and aggregation status; even a reviewed average is not expanded into independent transactions.

## Source events and the live Ponyta check

`reconcileSaleEvents` collapses repeated copies of the same provider event while retaining the evidence revisions. It joins Alt and eBay references only when there is one candidate from each provider for the same eBay item, event date and compatible grade/label, with no contradictory known certificates. It does not use price proximity to establish identity. Ambiguous repeated same-day events remain separate and flagged. Separate transaction IDs or different event dates under one listing ID survive. Multi-sale averages are retained separately, and potential overlap with individual sales is flagged.

The eleven shared Ponyta sales captured on September 5, 2026 reconcile to eleven events with both sources retained. All eleven Alt **reported price** amounts agree with eBay's native USD item amounts, including both Best Offers ($129.89 and $160). A follow-up fetch of all eleven Alt details found two disagreements in Alt's separate `usdAmount` field: item `336701223535` reports 120 but has USD amount 135; Best Offer item `127943558744` reports 160 but has USD amount 200. These mismatches remain in the fixture and event reasons. For the joined event's reported-price display, the function prefers eBay's native reported price; it does not use the conflicting converted field or average the two. Unknown shipping and fees are preserved.

`screenCompetitorSupply` is a supply-candidate gate, not a claim that the listing is an identity-matched comp. It excludes known current seller item IDs, rejects stale/invalid capture times from usable candidates, and keeps auction bids, unknown prices and unknown listing states out of asking-price candidates. Identity screening and explicit cohort matching remain necessary before using supply in #30.

## Reviewed comp decisions

Migration `028_add_slab_comp_decisions.sql` stores decisions keyed by evidence revision, valuation group and exact provider event/date. POST `/api/slab-comp-decisions` requires `slabId`, `identityRevision`, `revisionId`, `provider`, `providerId`, `date`, `decision` (`accept`/`exclude`), `note`, and optionally an explicit `itemPrice` with amount/currency. The request must originate in the application and is bounded to 16 KiB. The server verifies the exact source row and current confirmed identity, saves the decision and retains its evidence transactionally. Concurrent identity changes require reload. GET accepts `group` plus comma-separated `revisions` and returns only reviews for those references.

Decisions do not migrate automatically to new evidence revisions or corrected valuation groups. A reviewer may change a decision for the same revision; its reason and update time are retained with that review. Historical source revisions referenced by reviews survive cache cleanup. Review/history retention release belongs with the later product-history policy, not automatic deletion of referenced evidence.

Validation includes the Spanish Goldeen, misleading Low Pop number, CGC Gem Mint/Pristine/encoded grades, PSA 1 qualifiers, two-card bundles, conflicting extended-title labels, non-Latin names, wrong card numbers, currency abstention, fee/shipping arithmetic, reviewed-price overrides, own/stale supply and the eleven live source pairs. Isolated PostgreSQL tests verify source/date binding, identity revision conflicts, no inherited reviews on new evidence, retention and API origin/body limits. Run `npm test`, `npm run typecheck`, `npm run build`, and `node node_modules/tsx/dist/cli.mjs app/features/ebay-slab-pricing/screening/compDecisions.integration.test.ts`.
